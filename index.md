# Privacy Policy for SURGE HUNTER

Last updated: October 13, 2026

GENIUS GAMES operates the mobile game “SURGE HUNTER: BUY or PASS.”

## Information We Collect

SURGE HUNTER does not require users to create an account or log in.

The game does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

## Game Data

Game progress, scores, records, settings, and rankings may be stored locally on the user's device for gameplay purposes.

This information is not used by GENIUS GAMES to personally identify users.

## Financial Information

SURGE HUNTER is a simulation game.

The stock charts, prices, trading situations, and other market-related information presented in the game are fictional and are provided solely for entertainment purposes.

The game does not connect to brokerage accounts, execute real financial transactions, or provide financial or investment advice.

## Children's Privacy

SURGE HUNTER does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Third-Party Services

If third-party services are added to the game in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain what information those services collect and how that information is used.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

GENIUS GAMES may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its features change. Any changes will be reflected on this page together with an updated revision date.

## Contact
